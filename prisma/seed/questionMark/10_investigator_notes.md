# Investigator Notes --- Operation Question Mark

## Investigation question

Determine whether the four examination-paper leak incidents are
connected through a common network of people, organizations,
communications, financial activity, or logistics.

## Known facts

-   Four paper-leak incidents were reported between February and August
    2026.
-   SecurePrint Solutions appears in dispatch records associated with
    multiple incidents.
-   Arjun Mehta appears in examination logistics records and
    communication records.
-   Vikram Sethi appears in communications around more than one
    incident.
-   Neha Sharma appears in communications around EX-01, EX-02, and
    EX-03.
-   Several payments occurred near the dates of the incidents.
-   Vehicle movement records show logistics activity near relevant
    examination locations.

## Important uncertainties

-   The purpose of communications between Arjun Mehta and Vikram Sethi
    is not established.
-   Ownership of account `ACC-2098` has not been independently verified.
-   Vehicle presence near an examination location does not prove that
    examination material was transported.
-   The witness who saw WB12AB1234 did not identify who instructed the
    movement.
-   Digital circulation reports do not identify the original uploader.
-   No current evidence establishes a single mastermind or common
    organizer.

## Suggested investigative priorities

1.  Verify the beneficial owner of `ACC-2098`.
2.  Compare dispatch timestamps with examination-paper circulation
    timestamps.
3.  Verify the purpose and participants of repeated Arjun--Vikram
    communications.
4.  Confirm whether vehicles recorded near the incidents were carrying
    examination material.
5.  Review the relationship between MeritEdge Academy and people
    involved in the four incident periods.

## Investigator conclusion at this stage

There are several cross-case connections worth investigating, but the
current evidence supports a **lead**, not a conclusion of criminal
responsibility.
