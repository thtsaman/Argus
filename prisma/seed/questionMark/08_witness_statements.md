# Witness Statements

## Statement 01 --- SecurePrint warehouse employee

**Date:** 2026-02-15\
**Incident:** EX-01

The employee reported seeing a sealed examination-material packet moved
from the dispatch area to a vehicle identified as **WB12AB1234** on the
evening of 13 February. The employee recognized the vehicle from
previous dispatch work but could not identify who instructed the
movement.

**What the witness observed:** the packet, the vehicle, and the
approximate time of departure.

**What the witness did not observe:** the contents of the packet, any
exchange with Vikram Sethi, or any instruction to leak examination
material.

## Statement 02 --- Regional examination staff member

**Date:** 2026-04-23\
**Incident:** EX-02

The staff member reported that a digital copy of the paper was
circulating in a private group before the examination. The staff member
had no direct knowledge of who originally uploaded the document.

The staff member recalled that the name **Vikram Sethi** was mentioned
by another participant, but could not confirm whether Vikram had
uploaded, received, or merely been discussed in relation to the
document.

## Statement 03 --- Transport depot employee

**Date:** 2026-07-10\
**Incident:** EX-03

The employee confirmed that vehicle **OD05EF7788** arrived near the
Bhubaneswar regional depot on 8 July. The employee could not confirm
what material was inside the vehicle. The stated delivery purpose in the
route register was examination logistics.

## Investigator caution

Witness statements contain observations and recollections. They should
not be treated as proof of a person's involvement beyond what the
witness directly observed.
